Openings

Want to join us?

Our lab is always looking for motivated people. Interested students are welcome to apply at any time. There is no deadline, and we review applications as they arrive.

We welcome prospective Ph.D. and M.S. students, postdoctoral researchers, and OU undergraduates who want research experience.

Areas of interest

  • Battery management systemsModel-based and data-driven estimation, fast charging, and control of lithium-ion cells and packs
  • Next-generation batteriesModeling and control of silicon/graphite composite electrodes and all-solid-state batteries, with material-level characterization
  • Physics-informed machine learningLearning methods that work alongside first-principle models of energy and mobility systems
  • Autonomous and electric mobilityAutomated electric vehicles and battery-aware fleet operation for rural and tribal communities
  • Second-life batteriesDiagnosis, digital twins, and control for repurposed electric-vehicle batteries
  • Control and estimation theoryNonlinear, descriptor, and distributed-parameter (PDE) systems

Helpful background

Dynamic modeling, optimal control, optimization, system modeling and identification, and vehicle electrification. Experience with electrochemistry, battery testing, or machine learning is also valuable.

How to apply

Email Dong Zhang at dzhang@ou.edu with:

  1. CV
  2. Academic transcripts
  3. A one-page personal statement that says which areas interest you
  4. Copies of previous publications or writing samples
