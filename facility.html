Arbin Instruments LBT22043 Battery Cell/Module Testing System

  • 8 channels, up to 25 V / 20 A
  • CAN communication for hardware-in-the-loop testing
  • 3 automatic current-switched channels
  • Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS) capability
  • Auxiliary voltage and thermal sensor inputs
  • 0.02% FSR measurement accuracy
  • Real-world test profiles and life-cycle testing

Neware CT-4000 Battery Testing System

  • 24 channels, up to 5 V / 6 A
  • Designed for cylindrical and pouch cells
  • Independent constant-current and constant-voltage source on each channel
  • Real-world test profiles and life-cycle testing

ESPEC BTU-433 Criterion Benchtop Temperature Chamber

  • Temperature range of -20°C to 180°C
  • 4 cu. ft. testing room
  • Built-in pressure-relief blow-out port
  • Interior dimensions of 19.6" x 11" x 12" (W x D x H)
  • Touch-screen Watlow F4T programmer with Ethernet access

Gamry Interface 1010E (EIS)

  • Maximum current of ±1 A
  • Maximum potential of ±12 V
  • EIS frequency range of 10 µHz to 2 MHz

dSPACE MicroAutoBox II

  • IBM PPC 750GL 900 MHz processor board
  • Ethernet (TCP/IP) connection
  • 4 CAN channels

Universal Robots UR10 Collaborative Robotic Arm

  • Payload of 10 kg (22 lb)
  • Reach of 1300 mm (51.2 in)
  • Repeatability of ±0.1 mm
