Nov. 2026

Dr. Dong Zhang received the Rising Star Award from the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), presented at the ASME International Mechanical Engineering Congress & Exposition (IMECE).

Nov. 2026

The ACCEL-RT team attended the National Transportation in Indian Country Conference (NTICC) to engage with Tribal Nations and hear first-hand about the challenges of moving freight in rural and Tribal areas.

Oct. 2026

Dr. Dong Zhang was selected as Awards Chair of the ASME Energy Systems Technology Committee (ESTC). He will organize and administer the ESTC Best Paper Award competitions at future ASME ESTC venues.

SpotlightSep. 2026

A U.S. Department of Transportation delegation led by Assistant Secretary Seval Oz visited the University of Oklahoma to see our $20 million USDOT-supported ACCEL-RT initiative in action. The visit featured autonomous vehicle navigation on unmarked rural roads and interactive remote-assist driving, along with live field demonstrations of an automated drone emergency response system and the Roadway Intelligence Service digital twin, built to keep truck drivers safe, streamline logistics, and prevent freight delays along the I-40 corridor. ACCEL-RT project site

Sep. 2026

ESCL received $75,000 from the OU Office of Technology Commercialization to accelerate the technology transfer of our second-life battery testing, reuse, and control methods. The effort is led by Yuichi Kajiura.

Sep. 2026

Three papers were submitted to the 2027 American Control Conference (ACC): “Optimal Sensor Placement for Parameter Estimation in Li-ion Battery Packs Using a 1D-PDE Thermal Model” (Jorge Espin, Dylan Kato, Dong Zhang); “Physics-based Optimal Assignment of Heterogeneous Cells in Parallel-Connected Battery Packs” (Zhicheng Xu, Dong Zhang); and “Control-Oriented Modeling of Reversible and Irreversible Stack Pressure Growth in Cycled Lithium-Ion Pouch Cells” (Xinyu Yang and co-authors, with Dong Zhang).

Aug. 2026

Congratulations to Jorge Espin, whose paper “Adaptive State and Parameter Estimation for Parallel-Connected Batteries: A Descriptor System Approach With Experimental Validation” was accepted by IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology (TCST). Read the paper

Aug. 2026

Congratulations to Wonoo Choo, whose paper “Electro-Mechanical Model Predictive Charging Control for All-Solid-State Batteries via an Equivalent Maxwell Deformation Model” was presented at the IFAC World Congress in Busan, South Korea.

May 2026
